# Real-camera holdout

Place only new, unseen camera photos here. These files must never be copied into
the training dataset before the final model comparison.

Recommended minimum before production promotion:

- 15 paper milk cartons: KUN, LOF, MILO and at least two other brands.
- 15 hard negatives: cardboard boxes, newspapers, cans, bottles and pouches.
- Multiple backgrounds, lighting conditions, distances, rotations and crushed
  packages.

Use one subfolder per expected label. Do not use product-page images or frames
captured from the same video/session as training data.
